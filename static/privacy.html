Privacy Policy

How Solaris For The World collects, uses, and protects information — in plain language.

1. Information we collect

Solaris does not require you to sign up or create an account, so we do not collect usernames, passwords, or account profiles. When you use the site, we collect a limited amount of information tied to how the site operates:

  • Site usage data — pages visited, general device/browser information, and approximate location (derived from IP address), used to keep the service running and to understand how people use it.
  • Cookies — small files stored in your browser that support core site functionality and the advertising described below.

2. Cookies and third-party advertising technologies

Solaris is free to use because it is funded by advertising instead of subscriptions. To deliver and measure ads, we and our advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies.

Our current advertising network is Monetag. Monetag may set cookies and use similar technologies to display ads and measure their performance. In the future, we may add additional advertising partners, such as Google AdSense, to further support the free model — this page will be updated if and when that happens.

You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect ad display but will not prevent you from using Solaris's free generation tools.

3. How ads fund our free model and social causes

Every ad you watch on Solaris does two things: it keeps the platform free for everyone, and it helps fund humanitarian causes around the world. Revenue from advertising is split between running and improving the service and supporting the social projects described on our Impact page. In short — the ads you see are what make free, unlimited AI generation and our social mission possible.

4. Contact us about privacy

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach out and we'll respond as soon as we can: Email.

5. Your rights under GDPR

Solaris receives visitors from the European Union, so if you are located in the EU/EEA you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about your usage of the site.
  • Request deletion of that information.
  • Object to the use of cookies for advertising purposes, including by adjusting your browser or cookie settings, or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the email link above.
