Our Mission

We believe technology should be free for everyone — and that using it should make the world a better place.

Who we are

Solaris was born from a simple idea: AI is powerful, but it's locked behind paywalls that most people can't afford.

We built Solaris so anyone — anywhere in the world — can create with AI for free. No subscriptions. No credit cards. No limits on imagination.

Every time you generate an image, video or audio on Solaris, you watch a short ad. That ad pays for your creation — and 50% of what it generates goes directly to humanitarian causes around the world.

Our vision

🌍

Free AI for everyone

We live in a world where the best tools belong to those who can pay. We don't think that's right. Solaris is our answer.

🕊️

Technology for good

Every generation on Solaris is a small act of solidarity. You create. The world improves. It's that simple.

🏫

Real impact, real places

We focus on communities abandoned by their governments — rural schools, war zones, regions with no resources. We go where help is needed most.

🌍

Our long-term mission

We're constantly searching for new NGOs to partner with, so we can bring real benefits to people with limited resources.

Our long-term goal is to fund free schools through this AI — and to build prefabricated homes in rural areas, bringing technological progress to places their own country or state has left behind.

We're looking for NGOs to help us send aid to Gaza and other regions in armed conflict: medicine, food, and clothing for those affected.

We also support the protection of the Amazon, working to prevent damage from industrial companies in regions that are key to humanity's future.

Our vision: a world where we all coexist in peace, free of harm and prejudice, guided by loyalty and clarity as our principles.
